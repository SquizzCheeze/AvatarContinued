Avatar Continued Changelog
==========================

V1.14 (2026-11-18)

Added:

- Dressing Room (a new page in /avatar): try on any appearance in the game,
  collected or not, one slot at a time, and save the result as a look.
  Saved looks appear on the Outfits page and can be worn like any outfit,
  per specialization too. Looks are shared by all your characters.
- The Dressing Room can also try on the game's armour sets, a whole set at
  once.
- WoW Forever: the game only draws appearances your character could
  display (mostly your class's armour types). Those it won't draw are
  dimmed in the Dressing Room, and a slot holding one turns red.
