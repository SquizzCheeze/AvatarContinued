Avatar Continued Changelog
==========================

V1.11

Added:

- Reactions: your avatar can react to what happens. It dances when you
  finish a Mythic+ key or earn an achievement, plays a spell cast when you
  level up, and falls down when you die (getting back up once you are alive
  again), then returns to your chosen pose. Each can be changed or turned
  off under Animation in the settings, and /avatar anim <id> lets you try
  any other animation.
- A different outfit for each specialization. Tick the option under Outfits,
  choose a spec from the icons that appear, then pick its outfit - no need
  to switch spec to set each one up. The preview in the settings shows the
  chosen spec's outfit while your avatar keeps what it is wearing, and
  switching spec puts that spec's outfit on.
- Many more animations for the pose and for reactions: Wave, Cheer, Laugh,
  Applaud, Bow, Salute, Flex, Point, Roar, Shout, Cry, Shy, Kiss, Beg, Rude,
  Chicken, Sit, Kneel and Lie Down.
